# AURUM Refund Policy

Effective before the first sale. Applies to every digital product sold through an
AURUM checkout link (Stripe hosted checkout), including the Article 50(2) Evidence
Report, the Marking Readiness Pack, shellguard, and all B2B tool downloads.

## The policy

- **Full refund within 30 days, no questions asked.** If you are unhappy for any
  reason, you get your money back.
- To request a refund, email **derbaroser@gmail.com** with your order id or the
  email address used at checkout. No form, no justification, no argument.
- The refund is issued through the **original payment rail (Stripe)** back to the
  original payment method. We do not handle, store, or transmit card data.
- Refunds are honored without argument and processed within 3 business days of
  the request.
- If a product was not delivered, or a dated evidence report misses the agreed
  3-business-day delivery window, the refund is automatic on request.

## Scope

- One-time digital products and per-report services only.
- "Pay from EUR 1 / name your price" orders are covered by the same policy.
- This policy does not cover chargebacks filed with the bank first — contact us
  first and we will refund directly.

## Why this is written down

A buyer must know the refund terms before paying. This file is the canonical copy;
a short form of it appears in the footer of every product page that carries a live
checkout link. The terms above are the terms we honor.
